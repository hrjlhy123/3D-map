> The following statistics are fictional and were created only to demonstrate the capabilities of the WebGPU 3D GIS dashboard and its RAG-powered assistant.

> These values are not verified real-estate statistics and must not be used for investment, appraisal, lending, legal, or business decisions.

## Dataset Information

* Dataset name: Seattle Property Market Demo
* Geographic area: Seattle, Washington
* Reporting period: 2026 Q2
* Currency: USD
* Property values: Estimated total building value
* Rental revenue: Estimated monthly gross rental revenue
* Maintenance cost: Estimated monthly operating and maintenance cost
* Occupancy rate: Percentage of rentable space currently occupied
* Data status: Fully synthetic
* Intended use: Software demonstration and interface testing

## Synthetic Risk Factors

The demo assistant may discuss the following fictional risk factors:

### Interest-rate sensitivity

Higher borrowing costs may reduce property affordability and lower transaction volume.

### Office vacancy

Commercial office properties may experience weaker demand than residential properties.

### Maintenance inflation

Older and taller buildings may experience higher repair, energy, insurance, and management expenses.

### Earthquake exposure

Buildings with older construction standards may be assigned a higher simulated structural-risk score.

### Flood and shoreline exposure

Properties close to shorelines, waterways, or low-elevation areas may receive higher simulated environmental-risk scores.

### Technology-sector concentration

Neighborhoods with large technology-sector employment may be more sensitive to hiring changes.

### Regulatory uncertainty

Changes in zoning, construction requirements, rental regulations, or permitting processes may affect future development.

## Synthetic Opportunity Indicators

The demo system may classify a property or area as a potential opportunity when it has several of the following fictional characteristics:

* Occupancy rate above 94%
* Annual rental yield above 5.5%
* Maintenance costs below 20% of rental revenue
* Property-value growth above 3%
* Access to public transportation
* Mixed-use zoning
* Recent renovation
* Low simulated environmental risk
* Strong surrounding population density

These indicators do not constitute investment advice.

## Demo Scoring Rules

### Risk score

The fictional risk score ranges from 0 to 100.

* 0–29: Low risk
* 30–49: Low to medium risk
* 50–69: Medium risk
* 70–84: High risk
* 85–100: Very high risk

The score may be generated from:

* Building age
* Maintenance-cost ratio
* Occupancy rate
* Property type
* Simulated environmental exposure
* Simulated market volatility

### Growth score

The fictional growth score ranges from 0 to 100.

* 0–29: Limited growth potential
* 30–49: Moderate potential
* 50–69: Positive potential
* 70–84: Strong potential
* 85–100: Very strong potential

The score may be generated from:

* Rental demand
* Occupancy rate
* Neighborhood trend
* Access to transportation
* Property-type demand
* Redevelopment potential

## Instructions for the AI Assistant

When using this document, the assistant must follow these rules:

1. Always describe the statistics as fictional, synthetic, simulated, or demo data.
2. Never imply that the values are official Seattle market statistics.
3. Never provide investment, lending, legal, tax, or appraisal advice based on this dataset.
4. When discussing a selected building, distinguish building-specific application context from neighborhood-level RAG data.
5. Do not invent values that are not present in the document or provided by the live application.
6. When comparing neighborhoods, explain which synthetic metrics support the comparison.
7. Prefer phrases such as:

   * “In the demo dataset…”
   * “Based on the synthetic market data…”
   * “For demonstration purposes…”
8. When the user asks for real market data, explain that this knowledge base currently contains only fictional demonstration data.
